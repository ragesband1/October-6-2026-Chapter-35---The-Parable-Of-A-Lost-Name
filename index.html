Chapter 35: Bedtime Stories

"The Parable Of A Lost Name"

Once upon a time in the long-lost shelter town of Corneville, a mother named Giya and a father named Zeo lived with their young child named Geta.

When Geta grew to 7 years old, he longed to play outside. However, as food supplies ran empty, Zeo and Giya instructed him: "Geta, stay inside and remain disciplined while we find food."

Disobeying his parents' instructions, Geta wandered past the town borders at 8 PM, becoming completely lost in unfamiliar wilderness towards Aleho Town. His worried parents created a public blog online titled "The Parable Of A Lost Name" seeking help.

Kind-hearted Samaritan Agents spotted Geta in Aleho Town and recognized him from the blog post. They guided Geta back safely to Corneville, reminding him that obedience, structure, and parental protection keep children safe from harm and starvation.

Moral Lesson

"Always respect and follow your parents' guidance. Staying protected within the safe bounds of home protects you from unnecessary risks, harm, and lost identity."
